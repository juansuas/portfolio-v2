Student voices

In 2023, I processed primary students’ open-ended responses and visualized the results for the SICDIE dashboard. The study used LDA to explore what students valued and would change about school, and multilingual BERT to analyse how they felt about moving to secondary school. The paper discusses the limits of classifying emotions that can coexist in a single response.
