Chubut 2050 · Territorial scenarios

Chubut 2050 brings together population projections, education capacity and productive activity to explore regional planning decisions. The demo lets visitors compare departments and years and test scenarios with maps and simulators. It retains the original demographic and production data and uses fictional attendance, enrolment and school data in the education module.
